# Loom walkthrough (5–10 minutes)

Record the running app. Speak over these steps in order. Keep the load above 40,000 lb so the wind rule is visible if the forecast is windy.

1. Enter a real origin and pick a suggestion. Enter a real destination and pick a suggestion. Set the departure to the next hour or so. Enter a load over 40,000 lb, for example 42000. Leave spacing at 25 miles and plan the routes.
2. Show the three lines on the map. If the routing service returned fewer than three, read the note that says so.
3. Click a checkpoint. Read the mile, the ETA, the combined risk, and the wind, rain, and snow numbers.
4. Change spacing from 25 to 10 and plan again. Point at the checkpoint count on the cards and show that it went up.
5. Drag the corridor slider from hour 0 through hour 48. Say that the corridor colors change and the page does not reload or ask for a new route. Say that checkpoint risk is still the weather at the truck’s arrival time, not the hour on the slider.
6. Point at the Recommended badge. Say the order: fewest No Travel miles, then fewest Severe miles, then fewest High miles, then lowest average risk, then shortest travel time.
