# Loom walkthrough (5–10 minutes)

Record the running app, ideally the Render URL. Speak over these steps in order. Use a load over 40,000 lb and two cities more than 100 km apart, so the truck weight and the extra routes both matter.

1. Enter a real origin and pick a suggestion. Enter a real destination more than 100 km away and pick a suggestion. Set departure to the next hour. Enter a load over 40,000 lb, for example 42000. Leave spacing at 25 miles and plan the routes.
2. Show three distinct lines. Say that a long trip asks OpenRouteService for alternatives first, and if that is short of three it requests more routes through points off to the side. Say the load was sent as the HGV weight restriction. If a note says fewer than three came back, read it.
3. Point at the permanent label on each line: distance, travel time, and average risk. Hover a checkpoint and read the mile, the ETA, the combined risk, and the wind, rain, and snow numbers. Say the ETA follows the step times from the routing service, not one flat speed for the whole trip.
4. Change spacing from 25 to 10 and plan again. Point at the checkpoint count on the cards and show that it went up.
5. Drag the corridor slider, then press Play and Pause. Say the band is a radial gradient, it scales when you zoom, and the hours were already cached so the page does not ask for a new route. Press Hide corridor and Show corridor. Say checkpoint risk is still the weather at the truck’s arrival time.
6. Point at the Recommended badge and read the sentence under it. Say the order behind that sentence: fewest No Travel miles, then fewest Severe miles, then fewest High miles, then lowest average risk, then shortest travel time.
